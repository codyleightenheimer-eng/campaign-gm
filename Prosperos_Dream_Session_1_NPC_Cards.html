Session 1 — Quick Reference

NPC Cards

Every incidental NPC from Session 1's own reference table, condensed to what you actually need at the table — voice, hook, and what they'd say if asked.

Angus

Always

Fence & Information Broker — canyonheavy.market

"Heard you're new in-system. I can get you queue-jumped in ten minutes. Cost you a favor, not credits."

VoiceWarm, chatty, transactional. Never fully answers a question about himself — deflects with charm, not evasion.

What he'll actually sayPraises whichever storyline direction the crew leans toward. Routes his "ask" toward it without explaining why.

RememberHe's quietly Bratva intelligence. Don't reveal it tonight — let one thing he does later recontextualize this session in hindsight.

Loshe

Always

Dockmaster — the Dry Dock

Personally oversees every ship that comes into dock. Nothing happens on the Dream he doesn't hear about eventually.

VoiceBrisk, professional, not unfriendly — just busy. Talks while doing three other things.

What he'll actually sayAny rumor or piece of station news the table needs a nudge toward. He's your safety valve if the crew stalls.

RememberFree information, first time. If they come back for more, it should cost something — a favor, a job, a name.

Sem (Jury Semenov)

Always

Bartender — The Stellar Burn

Buy a drink, get a rumor. That's the whole business model.

VoiceEasy, low-key, unbothered. The kind of bartender who's heard everything twice.

What he'll actually sayOne genuine rumor per drink bought. Also manages retina-scan security for the club's private booths — knows who's meeting who upstairs, but won't say unless it's worth his while.

RememberA good spot to plant a Caliban graffiti mention or a Choke rumor if the table hasn't organically found one yet.

The Babushka

Always

Cybersurgeon — the Chop Shop

Legendary, intensely private. Lets her AI child Zhenya do the talking so she doesn't have to.

C:25 · Sawed-Off Combat Shotgun 2d10+2 DMG · I:90 · W:2(45)

VoiceClinical, few words. Warmth is there if you look for it, but she won't perform it for strangers.

What she'll actually sayAlmost nothing directly — Zhenya (holographic AI, appears as a small child) handles scheduling and small talk instead.

RememberAppointments are gated on good standing with Yandee's people. If the crew's on the outs with the Bratva, she won't see them — full stop, no exceptions tonight.

Reidmar

Option A

Union Rep, Teamsters Local — power broker

Legendary pilot, permanently impatient. Just had his freighters captured by the Stratemeyer Syndicate.

VoiceGruff, blunt, no patience for small talk — but listens closely when something's actually useful.

What he'll actually sayWants his ships back. Will use a new crew for exactly that if they prove capable tonight.

RememberDistributes Yandee's Sycorax to fund the Union's lobbying — an uneasy, real business relationship with the Bratva. Don't over-explain this tonight; just know it's true.

Trieu

Option A

Reidmar's daughter — Hangar Bay

Usually elbow-deep in a ship's guts, fixing whatever her father's fleet broke this week.

VoicePractical, a little guarded. Softens if someone shows genuine respect for her work.

What she'll actually sayTechnical, grounded information about ships and the Hangar Bay. A minor recurring face, not a major source.

Imogene Kane

Option B

Hunglung insurgency leader

Planning to retake ground from Tempest Co.'s guards — unaware she's dying because of what Bancali did to her.

VoiceIntense, focused, a leader who's used to being the smartest person in the room and usually is.

What she'll actually sayRecruiting pitch for the crew — wants volunteers to infiltrate the Ice Box through Caliban's Heart.

RememberHer "gills" (Bancali's experiment) are slowly making her sick and paranoid, unknown to her. Play the paranoia as a subtle character note, not an obvious symptom, tonight.

Paz Asis

Option B

Hunglung leader

Watches for authorities before making contact. Gets captured the day after meeting the crew.

VoiceNervous, watchful, speaks in short bursts and keeps checking exits.

What he'll actually sayWhatever the crew needs to know about the Choke situation — then vanishes.

RememberIf not rescued after capture, he'll give up the crew's plans under interrogation. A real countdown if you use him.

Dr. Bancali / Caliban

Option C

Unknown — do not name tonight

Graffiti. A nervous rumor. That's all this session gets.

VoiceN/A tonight — he doesn't appear as a character. He's a name half-scrawled on a wall.

RememberThe full truth — a father, a dying daughter named Ariel, a merger with the station itself — is a gut-punch reveal worth saving for when the crew has context to actually feel it. Resist the urge to explain anything tonight.
